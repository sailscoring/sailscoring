Boats scored under ORC are corrected using the course they actually sailed and the wind they sailed it in, so the scorer needs the marks, the courses and the breeze recorded alongside the finish times. Please fill this in as best you can — it is lower priority than your other race committee duties. Mistakes and omissions can be worked around using data from other sources.

Bearings and wind directions are MAGNETIC (°M) — exactly as read off your GPS, chart plotter or hand bearing compass. Do not convert anything.

Positions: write them as your device shows them. Degrees and decimal minutes is ideal — 53°23.45′N 006°04.12′W — but any format your device gives is fine.
